Our Methodology

Rigorous 4-Stage Automation Engineering Process

1. Architecture & FDS Specification

Comprehensive functional design specification (FDS), IO allocation, network topology mapping, and SIL risk analysis.

2. Logic Coding & Emulation

Structured IEC 61131-3 PLC programming with hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) digital twin simulation before deployment.

3. UL 508A Panel Build & FAT

Precision wiring in climate-controlled panel shop followed by rigorous 100% point-to-point Factory Acceptance Testing.

4. On-Site SAT & Commissioning

Live plant commissioning, multi-axis tuning, operator training, and 24/7 post-launch ramp-up engineering coverage.
