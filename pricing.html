Automation & Integration FAQ

How do you estimate turnkey automation project costs?

We conduct a detailed preliminary review of your User Requirements Specification (URS), calculate I/O counts, servo/drive requirements, robotics kinematics, and control panel scale, then provide a transparent, fixed-scope milestone proposal.

Do you supply complete electrical schematics and source code upon project completion?

Yes. We believe in complete client transparency. You receive 100% unlocked PLC/HMI/SCADA source code, fully commented IEC 61131-3 logic, AutoCAD Electrical panel drawings, and bill of materials (BOM) without proprietary lock-in.

How does your team guarantee minimal downtime during on-site commissioning?

We utilize digital twin simulation and hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) FAT testing at our facility to debug 95%+ of logical and communication exceptions prior to stepping foot on your factory floor.
